Attendance

Attendance is recorded when a student's card is scanned at the gate. Use the Daily list for one day, the Class register for a whole month (the broadsheet), or the School summary for every class. Days are Monday to Friday. Public holidays are not excluded yet.

Holidays for this month

Tap a day to mark it as a holiday, for example when the government declares one. The gate stops for that day, and it is left out of attendance counts. Tap it again to undo.

Staff

Staff Accounts

Create a staff account for each teacher. Staff will use their username and PIN to sign in to the teacher module. A PIN must be at least 4 characters. Disable an account to stop it working, without deleting its history.

Teaching Assignments

Assign each teacher to the classes and subjects they teach. A teacher signs in on the teacher page with their staff username and PIN, and can enter scores only for their own assignments.

Gate Duty Rotation

Pick up to 5 active staff for gate duty. The duty passes to the next person in this order each day, and starts again at the top after the last person.

Student Cards

Each student has a QR card. Scanning it at the gate records that student's attendance. A card's code can be reset if the card is lost. The old card then stops working.

Notices

Post announcements that appear on the home page for everyone to see. Unpublish or delete a notice at any time. Set an expiry date and it hides itself automatically.
